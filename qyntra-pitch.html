The Problem

You save everything.
You remember nothing.

  • 📁 Drive folders you haven't opened in months
  • ⭐ GitHub repos you starred and forgot
  • 📝 Notion pages buried three levels deep
  • 📧 Important emails lost in your inbox

The information is there. Just scattered, disconnected, and impossible to search across.

02 / 08

The Solution

One place for your knowledge.

Qyntra pulls from Google Drive, Gmail, GitHub, Notion — even your desktop — and turns it all into a private, searchable wiki you can actually talk to.

Ingest Everything

Connect sources once. Files, emails, repos, and pages flow in automatically.

Ask in Plain English

"What did I write about GraphRAG?" — it reads your files, not the internet.

Private by Default

Your data stays yours. No shared workspaces. No team noise. Just you.

Desktop Too

Pick a local folder and it scans every supported file recursively.

03 / 08

What It Does

Files

Grid and list views of all ingested files. Search by title, source, or tag. Real-time sync status.

Ask

Chat grounded on your corpus. Token counters, citation extraction, corpus stats sidebar.

Read

Full-text viewer for any file. Metadata, tags, source links. No download needed.

3D Galaxy

Interactive knowledge graph. Real files as nodes. Click to explore connections.

Sources

One-click OAuth for Drive, Gmail, GitHub, Notion. Toggle on/off per source.

Dashboard

Live stats, recent activity, connector health. See what's happening at a glance.
